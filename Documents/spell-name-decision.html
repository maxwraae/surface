Spell

Final pick for the focus-timer app name. May 30, 2026.

The name

Spell. One syllable. A real word. A spell of focus — the state of being absorbed, enchanted by the work, where time dissolves around you.

Why it works

  • Short and punchy — one syllable, easy to say, easy to type, easy to remember.
  • Evocative — focus as enchantment. You cast a spell. You're under a spell. The metaphor is rich and distinctive.
  • Not occupied — no major focus-timer app on the App Store uses "Spell" as its name. The AI agent tool "Spell" is a different category entirely.
  • Warm without being cute — it has a bit of magic to it, but it's not whimsical or precious.
  • Generates natural copy — "Cast a spell," "My spell is running," "Break the spell," "Start a spell."

The dual meaning

"Spell" also means orthography — how to spell a word. This is the only friction. But it's neutral (not negative), and it gives the name texture. The context makes the meaning clear: a focus-timer app called Spell is about enchantment, not spelling bees.

What was rejected

Dive

The prior turn's pick. Clean and instant, but Max didn't confirm — and re-assigning the goal is the signal. Also: "Focus Dive" exists on the App Store as a direct competitor, and "dive into work" is a productivity cliché.

Wick

The wick sustains the flame. Distinctive and warm, but the metaphor takes a half-second to translate (wick → flame → sustained focus), and "Wick" has an active study-planner app with a built-in focus timer. That translation delay is real friction.

Pulse, Hush, Ember

All have multiple existing focus-timer apps using the name. Crowded.

Sesh

Most natural to say ("start a sesh"), but too casual/slang for Max's taste. "StudySesh" also exists.

Bolt

Fast and decisive, but "bolt" as a verb means "flee" — wrong connotation. Also a to-do list app called Bolt.

Lock, Dash, Steep, Weld, Meld, Zone, Beam

All rejected on various grounds: existing competitors, wrong connotations, or not distinctive enough.

The search space

~40 candidates were generated across eight metaphor clusters (depth, fire, time/rhythm, sharpness, flow, stillness, commands, physics) plus abstract/distinctive words. Each was tested against: syllable count, instant recognizability, existing App Store competition, and fit with Max's taste (Aria-level thoughtfulness, not generic).

Bottom line

Spell is the one. It's distinctive, warm, punchy, and free. The dual meaning is texture, not friction.
